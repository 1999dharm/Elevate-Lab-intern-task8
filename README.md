Global Superstore Dashboard – Key Insights
1. Top-Performing Category: Technology
•	Technology has the highest total sales (INR 4.74M, 37.53% share).
•	It contributes more than 1/3 of total revenue, making it the top-performing category.

3. Best-Performing Region: Western Europe
•	Western Europe leads with INR 1.73M in sales, the highest among all regions.
•	Other strong regions include Central America and Oceania, with over INR 1M each.

5. Strongest Sales Month: December
•	December has the peak sales (INR 1.57M), closely followed by September and November.
•	This suggests a year-end boost – possibly due to holiday season or year-end deals.

7. Category with High Sales but Negative Profit: Furniture
•	Furniture (e.g., Tables) has high sales (INR 7.57L) but shows a loss of INR 64,083 in profit.
•	Indicates cost issues or discount-heavy strategy that’s hurting profit margins.
